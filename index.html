How the banker vote works

  • Round 1: remove the banker. Any member can start it. It passes only if every member except the banker votes yes. One no ends it.
  • Round 2: choose the next banker. Only the core family can hold the role. Everyone (except the outgoing banker) must pick the same person.
  • If the votes split, or not everyone votes in time, the role goes back to Liam Bondi.
  • Each round is open for 7 days. After a failed removal, a new vote can start after 24 hours.
  • The banker tools are frozen during round 2. Every vote, result and change of banker is on the ledger and emailed to the family.

Rules of the Bank

  • Camels move between accounts. Only the banker can create new ones, and every creation is shown on the ledger as a Mint and added to the total in circulation.
  • Every transaction is permanent and public. Mistakes are fixed with a reversal by the banker, never by deleting.
  • A payment that takes you below zero is allowed, but costs a 1-camel overdraft penalty to the Central Bank.
  • Illicit or dishonest camel dealings cost 3 camels, paid to the Central Bank and then shared out among the other accounts.
  • The ledger is sealed: each entry is chained to the one before it, so any edit made behind the bank's back shows up at the top of this page.

It is hereby notified that any unlawful or deceptive transactions involving camels shall incur legal penalties.

📲 Get the Camel Bank app

Add the bank to your home screen. It gets its own icon and opens straight to the bank, like an app. No app store needed.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the Camel Bank link in Chrome (not inside Gmail or Messages).
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (sometimes called Install app), then Add.

iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Open the Camel Bank link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Computer (Chrome or Edge)

  1. Click the ⋮ menu, then Cast, save and share → Create shortcut (tick “Open as window”).
  2. Or simply bookmark the page.

Page won't open on a phone? Open the link in a private/incognito tab. That fixes Google's “unable to open the file” error when several Google accounts are signed in.
